## Rules chosen (D, documented in the handler headers)
- Capture probes (a1, a2, a4) skip protected, invulnerable and cinematic-shielded fighters and offer only the nearest valid victim per tick, so one cast never captures two.
- a1: armor onHit in the definition plus the shared capture sequence armor. The capture hit is index 0 (five damage events, 26 HP).
- a2: startup armor, cinematic capture, capture hit deals 0, 69 HP at +72, release +78. The area (4 m + capsule radius, 3D, line of sight from the impact, max 3 others, nearest then lowest id, 20 HP U noncounterable R60 F6 U4) fires only while the cast still holds the capture at +72. Area hits use hit index 0 so they resolve before the final hit and survive a lethal final hit (the resolver orders by hit index). A miss spends the use (framework).
- a3: probes at +0 and +17 with per-index reaction (stun 30, then ragdoll 72). Armor from commit to the last active tick (armor startup plus armorUntil = activeEnd). Single target.
- a4: branch fixed at commit (an eligible fighter within 2 m: horizontal distance plus vertical capsule gap). Close probes the same 2 m reach through the active window. Ranged dashes 0.3 m per tick for 30 active ticks through `host.moveTogether`; a short step (wall) ends the dash. Sweep: sphere r 0.6 at capsule centre +0.2 Y from the previous to the new centre against the target capsule. The ranged capture hit deals 0 and 27 HP lands at +36. Armor only after capture.
