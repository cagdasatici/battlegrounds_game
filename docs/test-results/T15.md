# T15 Vanguard awakening (sv.a1 to sv.a4): test report

Date: 2026-11-01. Branch claude/busy-faraday-hqbvp3 line, on top of T14 (a403214). Subcommits T15.1 to T15.4.

## Scope
- sv.a1 Stillpoint Reversal (lethalCounter), sv.a2 Skybreak (groundWave), sv.a3 Horizon Break (longBeam), sv.a4 Orbit Collapse (cinematicGrab).
- All numbers are D. Definitions are in packages/shared/src/config/kits/sv.ts, handlers in apps/server/src/kits/sv/*.ts.
- sv.a1: commit spends the form and frees the caster at once; the 600 tick counter is a private status
  (per runner map plus `Fighter.lethalCounterUntil`), not an action. Trigger = cinematic capture of the attacker, damage 100 HP
  (10000 hundredths, never Infinity) at trigger+60, release at +72, recovery 18, heal to 100 HP on damage. Absorb (over 40 m 3D, wall, attacker
  or caster already captured) ends the status with no damage, heal or recovery.
- sv.a2 / sv.a3: grounded only, invulnerable from commit through the last active tick, single query at activeStart, max 4 nearest first.
- sv.a4: shared capture sequence (td/sequence.ts) with cinematic capture, release offset 96, damage 100 HP at +90, heal to full.

## Limitations
- The a1 status is private server state; there is no owner-only replication or HUD countdown yet (`lethalCounterRemaining` is the hook).
  No on-screen check was run. Physical iPad checks: NOT RUN.
- Only sv.a2 is covered end to end over the wire. sv.a1, sv.a3 and sv.a4 are covered by tick driven unit tests with a fake host (a wall is a plane).
- The 100 HP damage cap equals a full health bar, so every damaging awakened hit is lethal against a full bar unless it is refused; the survivor
  and release paths are exercised with fixtures (protection or invulnerability set on the victim).
- Clients are not given a1 trigger/absorb events; the cinematic uses the existing capture and hit events only.
