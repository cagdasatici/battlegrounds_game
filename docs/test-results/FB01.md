# FB01 iPad selection overlay suppression

Owner report 2026-11-04: rapid button presses on iPad trigger select-all and the blue selection overlay.

## Change
- `apps/client/src/base.css`: `user-select:none`, `-webkit-touch-callout:none`, transparent tap highlight and `touch-action:manipulation` on html/body; `user-select:text` restored for `input, textarea, [contenteditable]`. Canvas keeps `touch-action:none`.
- `apps/client/src/app/SelectionGuard.ts` (mounted in `boot`, cleanup registered): `preventDefault` on `selectstart`, `contextmenu`, `dblclick`, `gesturestart` unless the target is editable; `selectionchange` clears stray selections unless a field is focused.
- Viewport meta unchanged: iOS 10+ ignores `user-scalable=no`; `touch-action:manipulation` removes double-tap zoom.
