# QS01 Original kit and defensive audio

Node v24.22.2, Chromium (headless, real WebAudio). Decisions: 0042. Audibility judgments are delegated, not a human sign-off. Status: IMPLEMENTED, DEVICE CHECK PENDING.

## Changed behavior (presentation only)
- `synth.ts`: new Stone (thud then three falling chip ticks), Tide (noise sweep then descending tone) and Ember (rising charge then dry ignition crack) cast recipes; three element loop beds (`LOOPS`), `loopKindFor`, `SUSTAIN_MIN_TICKS`. Block gain raised so it is not the quietest sound. Counter, KO, hit, heal unchanged.
- `AudioBus.ts`: loops (`startLoop`, `setLoopDistance`, `stopLoop`, `stopAllLoops`) count toward the 16 voice cap and share the steal rule; `suspend`/`resume`, `ensureRunning`, context `statechange` handler stops loops when the context leaves running (iOS interrupted); one-shots refused while suspended.
- `Sfx.ts`: own eventId dedupe ring, per-frame `scanSustainAudio` from replicated state and tick, visibilitychange and pagehide handling, effects volume re-read, early unlock when the page already had user activation (Play press precedes match attach), gesture listeners kept until the context is running, read-only `__ebAudio` probe removed on teardown.

## Assessment (delegated, not a human sign-off)
From the measurements: every sound peaks between 0.1 and 0.8 without clipping; the three elements are separated in spectrum and time shape (Stone is sub-100 Hz with ticks, Tide a swept mid band, Ember a rising saw then a bright crack); block stays short, bright and distinct from counter and KO. Weakest points: Block is quiet by RMS (0.008) compared with casts, and Tide and Block overlap most spectrally; Stone is low and may be inaudible on iPad speakers (little output below 150 Hz), so its chip ticks carry the identity there. These are the items for QD01.
