# QV03 Fault Strike signature

Base SHA: 027e3b4. Node v24.22.2, pnpm 10.28.0. Chromium via SwiftShader, 1024x768, effective tier low.

## Changed behavior
- `apps/client/src/fx/kits/sv.ts`: sv.b1 gets a custom Active effect, `playFaultStrike`, of exactly 24 particles in three distinct layers (colour, size and life differ):
  - contact: 4 bright short-lived (0.18 s) crack flashes at the 3 m direct-strike impact point;
  - split: 8 chips (0.4 s) leaving the impact in two diverging rows forward; end points lie within the handler wave box (2 m wide, 8 m forward);
  - settling: 12 dark fragments resting on the ground along the footprint, life 0.9 s, outlasting the chips; positions without a surface are skipped.
  If the impact point has no surface (void) nothing spawns.
- Windup is unchanged: the generic grounded decal ring plus the QV02 retained warning. Impact on a confirmed `hit` is unchanged.
- Small shared change (outside the row's file list, in its spirit): `fx/kits/common.ts` gains an optional `Recipe.playActive` hook and exports `SurfaceHeight`; recipes without it behave as before.
- `tests/unit/fx-placement.test.ts`: its fracture-style placement case used sv.b1; switched to sv.b4 (same generic fracture style) because sv.b1 no longer uses it.
- Rig clips (`rig.ts`) not changed; the QF01 weight-shift anticipation key stays.

## Source mapping (sv.b1)
Shipped id sv.b1 Fault Strike, handler faultStrike (apps/server/src/kits/sv/faultStrike.ts). Origin = caster pose at activeStart; facing = replicated facingYaw; direct range 3 m; wave box 2x2x8 at activeStart (branch B, only for targets not directly struck); active ticks 24..28, recovery 24; blockable U direct / B wave; impact event = bus `hit` with moveId; cleanup = particle lifetimes (max 0.9 s), warning removed by AttackTelegraphs on phase end/interruption/death/leave. Pose at warning/contact/recovery comes from the existing QF01 clip (unchanged).
