# UX10 combat visual effects

Client-only presentation work. No server combat logic, damage, timing or hitboxes changed. Every effect is driven by a
confirmed server combat event or a replicated action phase and expires by render-time life (no timers, no damage handlers).
All art is procedural Three.js (additive spheres, rings, boxes, point lights); nothing is copied.

Evidence: `docs/test-results/UX10/*-before-*.png` (captured on the unmodified code with the same scripted lab) and
`*-after-*.png`. Lab: `tests/browser/harness/fx.ts` (real GameScene + real presentation modules + fake bus),
`tests/browser/ux10-shots.spec.ts`. SwiftShader software WebGL, not a device. iPad checks: NOT RUN.

## UX10a shared impact language

Every confirmed `hit` now draws: a white-hot additive flash plus a larger element-tinted bloom, a radial spark burst
(additive streaks plus short fat "chunk" streaks), an expanding ground shockwave ring, a blast disc facing the blow, and a
brief point light pulse (Medium/High only). Power scales with server damage and class; finishing blows (m1.4, or the hit that
leaves 0 HP) are heavier; a KO event draws an oversized flash, three staggered shockwaves, a rising light pillar and a spark
fountain. Block draws a distinct cyan shield ripple (three staggered rings on the guard plane, dome flash, deflected sparks).
Counter draws a gold flash with crossed rings. Camera shake is `shakeFor(power)` and never weaker than the old plan; it is
skipped under Reduced Motion, which also halves flash brightness and disables point lights.

## UX10b per-ability signatures

Each base (b1-b4) and awakened (a1-a4) ability of the three fighters is a composition of one windup style (gather, vortex,
pillar, coil, sigil), one active style (slash, ribbon, column, wave, beam, orbit, spiral, nova) and one impact style
(shards, splash, embers, geyser, crack, flare), tinted by the element theme (stone ochre/amber/slate, water aqua/deep/foam,
ember orange/gold/red). The 24 combinations are unique (unit-tested). Other kit moves (x slots, activations) get an
element-flavoured fallback. Windup glows are owned by the caster: they end on interrupt, death, hand-over to Active or room
leave. Windup length follows the ability's `startup` ticks (display only). Beam visuals are capped at 6 m so they never
claim a footprint the server did not grant. The M1 finisher (m1.4) draws a swing trail on the replicated Active phase, a
charge flash on Windup, and an element-tinted crossing slash plus shards/splash/embers on the confirmed hit. The Ember Orb
(ProjectileViews) gets a launch burst (flash, ring, sparks, light) and a glowing two-layer trail every 0.3 m of travel,
cyan after a reflection, and a fizzle flash when it ends.

What each looks like (verified by looking at the screenshots listed below, SwiftShader):
- Fault Strike (stone): three concentric yellow sigil rings contract on the ground with white vertical light posts, then a
  forward ground wave, white-hot flash and cracks/shards on impact.
- Rising Pillar (stone): ring contracts and posts rise; active is a tall column of white-yellow light over a flash and ground ring.
- Undertow Spiral (water): cyan vortex dashes swirl up around the caster, then a helix of cyan/white streaks with blue droplets.
- Mirror Current (water): expanding cyan nova rings; hit is a cyan bloom with a ground ring.
- Cinder Array / Furnace Ray (ember): orange gathering orb with converging streaks and a forward-facing ring coil; thick
  yellow/white ribbons on the active; orange bloom and rising embers on impact.
- Ember Orb: orange launch burst, glowing trail behind a wireframe orb. M1 finisher (ember): orange blast with crossing slash.
