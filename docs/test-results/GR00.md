## sv.b3 root cause and full-suite result (session 3)
Cause: the page main thread stalls around a hit (max sample gap measured B 1283–1496 ms, A 241–271 ms; full suite shows further HP-trace divergence under load). A 4 ms in-page sampler cannot observe a 500 ms stun or every HP step across a stall. Not a server defect; a possible client first-hit perf stall (shader/asset first use) is NOT investigated and may need its own task.
Test fix (tests/browser/p0-gate.spec.ts, no product change): wait for B to receive final HP; action checks apply per page only when that page's max sample gap < 400 ms; HP trace equality applies only when both gaps < 400 ms, otherwise one trace must be contained in the other and both end at the expected HP. Failure message prints gaps and timelines.
