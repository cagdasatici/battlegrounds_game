## Rework round 1 (warning/impact lane)
Base: 50f2a1c. Same method, same limits (desktop Chromium on SwiftShader, stills, medium spoofed). All 144-plus JPEGs and `bridge-metrics.json` regenerated.

Changes (presentation only; no damage or timers touched):
1. Fault Strike windup footprint (D-QI01-1): `AttackTelegraphs.ts` fill is now dark saturated red (windup 0x8f1d0c, active 0xd01808) at opacity 0.4 to 0.75 (0.6 flat on low and Reduced Motion, was 0.18) with a light edge (0xfff0a0 / white) instead of a pale fill on pale planks. Impact: crack flash 4 to 6 particles, size 0.3 to 0.9, life 0.38 s; settling fragments size 0.16 to 0.28, life 1.1 s (`kits/sv.ts`, still inside the recipe budget: 26 active particles).
2. Furnace Ray (D-QI01-2): body particles are about 3 to 4 times larger (core 0.5, sheath 0.75, flare 0.6), spaced denser near the caster, emit every 4 ticks with 0.12 s life (`kits/ea.ts`), live peak 56 plus flares, inside the low cap. Root cause of the missing low-tier beam: `EffectsPool.update` advanced particles by the whole frame dt. Under the slow frame rate of the CPU rasteriser a frame took longer than the 0.1 s beam life, so each beam particle died in its first update before it was ever drawn; Furnace Ray is the shortest-lived effect and so was the one that vanished. `update` now caps one step at 0.05 s (`MAX_STEP_SEC`), so every particle is drawn at least twice. This applies to all effects and to every tier (a slow frame can no longer erase an effect unseen).
3. Move-name tags (D-QI01-3): `separateTags` (pure, `CombatTextFormat.ts`) stacks overlapping cast tags vertically with a 4 px gap; `CombatText.ts` applies it each frame to `cast` tags only.
4. Spec capture (evidence only): the Furnace Ray beam shot is now taken 0.8 s after the key press (polling could never catch a 0.5 s windup plus 1.5 s beam through three SwiftShader screenshots), impact shots take the defender first and alone.

Tests that fail if a fix breaks (all in `pnpm test`): `attack-telegraphs.test.ts` (QI01 dark fill, light edge, opacity floors, luminance contrast against sand), `fx-ea.test.ts` (beam width, density at 6 m, and a pooled particle surviving one 0.4 s frame; fails without the dt cap), `combat-text-format.test.ts` (three coincident tags end with no overlap, separated tags untouched). Existing drain tests now step the pool in 0.05 s increments.
