Device label: desktop Chromium on SwiftShader (CPU raster), dev client, 1024x768. Diagnostic only, NOT a device, not full speed.

## What was captured
New `tests/browser/quality-bridge.spec.ts` (workers 1). Real host plus two guests over real auth and a real Colyseus sparring room: Stone Vanguard (host), Tide Duelist, Ember Artificer (awakened). East bridge (x about 22 to 30, z 0). Successive exchanges, each with real held key input:
1. Vanguard Fault Strike (sv.b1) on the Duelist, 1.8 m.
2. Duelist Undertow Spiral (td.b2) on the Vanguard, 1.7 m.
3. Artificer Furnace Ray (ea.a3) on the Duelist, 6 m.
4. Three-way overlap: all three on the bridge, all three signatures cast in the same moment.
Four configs: normal/low, normal/medium, Reduced Motion/low, Reduced Motion/medium. 144 JPEGs in `docs/test-results/QI01/` named `<config>-<exchange>-<attacker|defender|observer>-<phase>.jpg` (exchange 4: `...-three-way-<kit>-overlap-<early|mid|late>.jpg`), plus `QI01/bridge-metrics.json` (per shot: ms since key press, server tick, caster action at capture, FX pool count, draw calls, triangles; per exchange: server-confirmed hit, FX peak, drained).
Compared at the same scale with Q00 (`Q00/east-medium-1024x768.png`, `Q00/east-low-1024x768.png`; same 1024x768, production CameraRig, 60 degree FOV). Q00 plates are a static free camera, the QI01 plates are the in-game shoulder camera, so framing differs but landmark scale is comparable.

Fixtures (synthetic, labelled): placement and camera yaw, HP and cooldown resets, awakening meter, and for medium a spoofed WebGL renderer string (the ordinary client pins SwiftShader to low, so medium here is commanded, as in Q00/QE01). Damage, hits and phases are server decisions.

## Automated results
- All four configs: PASS (4 passed, 3.5 min). Every exchange had a server-confirmed hit for its move (sv.b1, td.b2, ea.a3, and at least one hit in the overlap), FX pool drained to 0 after every exchange, no console or page errors on any of the three clients.
- Renderer (max over captures): normal/low 73 calls, 121,552 triangles; normal/medium 84 calls, 149,246 triangles; Reduced/low 74 calls, 121,552; Reduced/medium 85 calls, 149,578. All inside 120 calls and 150,000 triangles; medium is within 0.3 percent of the triangle ceiling (no margin). FX pool peak 232 of capacity in the overlap.
- Phase labels are triggers, not exact timestamps. Under three SwiftShader clients a screenshot takes 0.1 to 10 s, so "windup" frames often show the caster already Idle (see the `action` field per shot). Treat the stills as representative of the effect, not as frame-exact phase captures. FX peak per exchange is sampled between screenshots and varies run to run (for example Fault Strike peaked 100 in one run and 12 in another), so it is not a Reduced Motion comparison.
- Full-speed clips: NOT RUN. A video attempt produced three unlabelled 3 to 4 MB WebM files and was discarded; the spec has no video.
- U fx-placement, fighter-model, arena-visuals: in `pnpm test` below.
