## Limitations (actual)
- Not run: physical iPad checks (NOT RUN). No latency/jitter matrix (A07/A15 style runs with RTT 20 to 250 ms) was run for these moves.
- Only td.b1 has an over-the-wire test. b2, b3 and b4 are covered by the tick driven unit tests (fake host plus real `World` for b2 wall
  motion and b3 wall contact detection), not by real clients.
- Real projectile reflection is pending: T19 provides the projectile system. td.b4 only calls an optional `ProjectileProvider.reflect`;
  acceptance for reflect-once, second-reflection absorb and no tunnelling through walls is NOT verified here (A10, A19 pending).
- `moveTogether` sweeps a lifted capsule in a straight line against static colliders and stops the group at the first contact; it does
  not slide along walls and does not follow slopes vertically (the next locomotion step re-settles y). Only flat floor and vertical
  walls (wall.0/wall.1) were exercised.
- The P1 elevated slam is implemented but disabled (`P1_ELEVATED_SLAM = false`) and only unit-tested with a fake surface height.
- Interaction with the Vanguard handlers (T12) was not tested; they were developed in parallel and may touch the same framework files.

## Resolved ambiguities (all D)
- Capture sequence timing: scheduled offsets are relative to the capture tick (b4: trigger tick); release at lastOffset + 1; recovery
  (18/24/18/30) starts after the release.
- b2 motion: 36 equal steps on ticks +1..+36; a short step stops all later motion.
- b2 elevated landing: caster grounded on any tick in (capture, capture + 48).
- b3: capture hit deals 0, 9 HP is the +18 hit; marker valid on release + 1 .. release + 89 while the victim is still the same ragdoll
  (any new action, landing out of ragdoll, death or captor death ends it); bonus attributed to the captor, the 5 point hit to the
  thrown victim; the captor and other thrown bodies are not collision targets; the bonus is also consumed by the first player contact.
- b4: armor covers the whole stance window and the response; an absorbed hit or reflected projectile ends the stance immediately and
  the full 30 tick recovery follows; after a response the recovery follows the release.
- `cancelCurrent` now clears `counterOpenUntil` so a stance never outlives the action that opened it.
