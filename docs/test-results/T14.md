# T14 Awakening system: test report

Date: 2026-11-01. Branch claude/busy-faraday-hqbvp3, on top of the merged T11, T12 and T13 work.

## Scope
- `Fighter.meter100`; death and respawn reset meter, form, use bits and contextSpent (spec 11, 15).
- `apps/server/src/sim/Awakening.ts` (`AwakeningSystem`): damage based meter, activation, form state, form expiry.
  - Gain = actual HP removed in hundredths from a hit or capture commit by a Base-form attacker. No gain from blocked, countered,
    overkill, self, practice (`meterEnabled=false`), while Awakened, or during activation. Capped at 20000 (sv) or 35000.
  - Activation (never buffered): Idle, grounded, unprotected, Base, full meter, else METER / FORM / STATE / INVALID.
    `sv.activate`: 30 ticks, armor, no invulnerability, no damage, no heal. `td.activate`: 60 ticks, armor, invulnerable ticks 48 to 59,
    burst at +48 (20 HP, radius 5, U, armorBreak, noncounterable, ragdoll 72, F6 U6, max 4, nearest first), heal 40 once at completion.
  - Completion: form Awakened, meter 0, use bits reset, contextSpent false, formEndTick = completion + 2940 for td (none for sv).
  - Form expiry returns to Base on the exclusive tick; it never cancels a committed cast or touches captures (A14).
  - An interrupted activation (armorBreak, capture, death) keeps the meter and stays Base (D, spec silent).
- `packages/shared/src/awakening.ts`: `meterRequired100`, `meterFraction` (threshold normalised HUD value).
- `ArenaRoom`: `awaken` edge, activation result with castId and cast event, burst candidates merged into serial resolution, meter gain
  after resolution, form/meter/bits/formEndTick replicated in the public player record, cleanup on leave, death and dispose.
- `ActionMachine`: `beginAwakening`, `Awakening` expiry.
