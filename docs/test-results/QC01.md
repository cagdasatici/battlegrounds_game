## Implemented
- `apps/client/src/scene/CameraRig.ts`: shoulder framing. When an opponent is within 2.5 m and hidden behind the local torso (measured from the unshifted camera), the pivot and boom slide up to 0.55 m camera-right, eased linearly over 0.15 s, latched until separation above 3 m. The right side is sphere-cast with the existing cast radius and clearance; an obstructed right shoulder gives zero offset, and an obstruction appearing mid-offset clamps at once. Yaw, pitch, distance setting and capture-track yaw/distance are untouched. Reduced Motion: constant collision-clamped 0.55 m shoulder while a target exists, no easing, no switching, track cancelled (interpretation, see conflict below). Pure helpers `fighterMarkers`, `projectPoint`, `torsoBlocks`, `pointVisible`, `framingOk`.
- `apps/client/src/app/GameLoop.ts`: passes the nearest living remote fighter (presented pose) or null when the local fighter is Dead, spectating or alone; stores `framing` for the debug probe.
- `apps/client/src/main.ts`: debug-only `setCameraYaw` and `snapshot().camera` (offset, pose, head and both shoulder markers, projected screen positions, visibility).
- Tests: 9 new unit tests in `tests/unit/camera-prediction.test.ts` (framing, ease, hysteresis, obstructed shoulder, mid-offset obstruction, death/spectator, Reduced Motion with track, track ownership). New `tests/browser/quality-camera.spec.ts`: real host and guest, real held Attack, centre/wall/bridge, aligned/right/left stances. Marked head and shoulder captures for windup, impact and recovery on both sides are in `docs/test-results/QC01/`.

## Conflict needing owner decision
With a 6.5 m boom and an opponent about 1.5 m past the local torso, a 0.55 m lateral shift moves the opponent's head only about 0.10 to 0.15 m off the torso axis. A head centre test cannot pass for aligned or camera-left opponents at that offset, and a rightward-only shift cannot reveal the left side (the plan also forbids automatic lateral switching). The torso silhouette radius 0.25 m is my own D value. Options for the owner: raise the maximum offset or allow height/pitch assistance, accept partial head visibility as the predicate, or allow side switching. One adjustment inside the slice (staggered fixtures) was made and the ledger row stays IN PROGRESS per plan rule 8.
