## Decisions and limitations
- Measured jump apex is ~1.40 m (analytic 1.33 m + discrete first step without gravity); accepted as D.
- `moveX/moveZ` are treated as a world-space horizontal direction; the client must rotate its camera-relative
  stick vector by camera yaw before sending (spec 7 says "camera relative" vector; documented choice).
- Facing lock on (camera yaw 720°/s) not implemented; only facing lock off behaviour.
- Non-jump edges are drained and dropped until the action machine (T09) exists.
- No void/ring-out handling: a player falling off keeps falling (death/respawn is a later task).
- No client prediction (T08). Overload-based join refusal is not wired here.
