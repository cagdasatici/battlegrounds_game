## Scope
- `apps/server/src/sim/ActionMachine.ts` (pure): per-fighter action state (spec 12) with exclusive integer
  tick deadlines and a cast `generation` token; dash classification (forward/side/back, ties forward/back,
  stationary = forward), separate cooldown buckets (forward 300, side shared 120, back 240), evasive
  (ragdoll age ≥ 12, cooldown 1800, invulnerable first 8 ticks, stationary uses facing right, clears landing
  bonus), guard (Idle + blockHeld + grounded, live after 3 startup ticks, 3 tick release recovery, 6 tick
  blockstun keeps guard), stun/ragdoll reactions, ragdoll expiry (airborne stays ragdolled, grounded → 12 tick
  recovery), generic Windup/Active/Recovery cast phases (for T10/T11), death, respawn (cooldowns reset,
  spawn protection 120). Forward dash and cast commits cancel spawn protection.
- `apps/server/src/sim/HitResolver.ts` (pure): candidates sorted by toi, attacker ID, cast counter, hit index
  (target ID as final tie); serial order: stale/dead attacker, dead target, per cast per victim-life hit cap,
  spawn protection, invulnerability, cinematic capture shield, counter, guard (120° cone, `>= cos 60°` with
  1e-9 tolerance, zero vector frontal, B only, zero chip), capture commit, damage (integer hundredths, clamped)
  and reaction (armor ignores reaction unless armorBreak). `HitLedger` pruned when the cast ends.
- `apps/server/src/sim/CaptureRegistry.ts`: one captor per victim, tied to captor cast generation; release on
  final hit, deadline expiry, stale captor generation, death and departure.
- `apps/server/src/sim/Combat.ts`: room `CombatSystem` combining the above; victim death/departure puts the
  captor into 18 tick recovery; captor death/departure/interruption frees the victim; `invariantViolations()`.
- `ArenaRoom.stepTick`: combat expiries, then dash edges → `requestDash` with `actionResult` reply
  (accepted/reason) to the owner, guard update from `blockHeld`, locomotion lock (Stunned/Ragdolled/Captured/
  Dead/Dash) and dash velocity override via a new optional `overrideOf` param on `MovementSystem.step`;
  action/moveId/actionStart/End/hp100/protectedUntilTick replicated. Fighter added on ready, removed (with
  capture release) on leave, disposed with the room. No setTimeout or second scheduler.

## Decisions, conflicts and limitations
- Conflict: spec 12 says captured fighters take third-party damage "only where the capture handler permits";
  spec 13 says default capture is not immune to ordinary third-party damage. Chosen: default captures permit
  third-party damage (no reaction while captured); `cinematic` captures shield both participants.
- D: captor death/departure/interruption releases the victim to Idle (spec names only the victim-gone case).
- D: invulnerable/protected/shielded hits do not consume the per-target hit entry; blocked and countered do.
- D: forward dash counts as an offensive request (cancels spawn protection); side/back/evasive do not.
- Not yet networked: hit candidates, captures and counters have no producers until T10/T11/T13, so A04/A08
  and capture cleanup are verified by deterministic unit tests, not over sockets. Forward-dash hitbox, M1,
  input buffering and combat event broadcast are T10+ work. No KO/respawn flow in the room yet (T17).
