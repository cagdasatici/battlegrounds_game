## Limitations
- The browser suite is timing sensitive in this sandbox: different specs failed on different attempts with the shipped T20 code (attempts 1 and 2 and two partial runs), then passed 34 of 34 on the final full run and 4 of 4 on a repeated single spec. No failure touched an Artificer path (the suite never selects the kit). The single baseline probe passed, so a T20 effect is not proven impossible, only not observed; a flaky-test review belongs to a separate task.
- Playwright rewrites evidence files under docs/test-results (png and json); they were restored with `git checkout -- docs/test-results` after every run.
- Physical iPad checks: NOT RUN. No client rendering of the Artificer, its effects or its HUD (kit hidden, client untouched).
- Wire tests use a server fixture to switch the seat to the Artificer; the Artificer is not selectable in P0.
- Orb aim is horizontal yaw only (decision 0010). Vent Rush air pitch is read from the validated input packet at commit; not exercised over the wire (unit only).
- Gaps filled with D values are listed in decision 0014 (intermediate Cinder Array stun 12, Furnace Drop aim point and 4 target cap, Vent Rush recast threshold).
- Finisher heal, cooldown reset and meter specifics for the Artificer are T22, awakening is T21.
