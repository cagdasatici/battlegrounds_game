# 0041 QV09 universal attacks and contextual outcomes (delegated design calls)

Owner delegated design decisions to the orchestrator (decision 0033). Ratings are capture-based, delegated, not a human sign-off.

1. Scope of the table: `HitFx.planFor` now keys on the replicated `moveId`. `m1.1` keeps the original light spark (existing tests unchanged). `m1.2` adds a wide directional spray, `m1.3` a small palm ring, `m1.4` (with or without the finisher heavy flag) a forward fan, ring, hitstop 4 and shake 0.3. A heavy flag on m1.1 to m1.3 (Awakened attacker) keeps the heavy base. Kit ability ids have no variant, so generic output is unchanged and kit recipes layer on top ("kit overrides win" means kit recipes are never suppressed or replaced).
2. Shared context moves are recognised only as `sv|td|ea.wall`, `.pickup`, `.throw`. Wall: grey-brown dust burst plus wide ring, hitstop 3, shake 0.25. Urn throw: ochre burst and ring. Pickup (a `cast` event): ochre gather motes. Nothing is invented for events the server does not send.
3. New generic kinds: `capture` (small pale tether ring, 16 particles), `release` (pale burst plus ring), `respawn` (rising pale motes, ring, short flash on the respawned fighter). Block gains 5 cyan sparks over the shield arc. Counter and KO are unchanged. Text: `CAUGHT` and `FREE` tags over the held fighter (CombatTextFormat), subject to the existing combat text filter and Reduced Motion rise rule.
4. Dash, evasive and guard have no combat event, so they are drawn from replicated state in `scanMotion` (one frame callback per attached HitFx, cancelled in the disposer). Dash kind comes from the server moveId (`dash.forward|side|back|evasive`); trail direction uses replicated velocity when above 0.5 m/s, else facing, side or back. One cue per replicated action start; guard plays only on entry. Budgets: dash 8, evasive 14, guard 5 particles.
5. Settings: flash uses the existing `flashLevel`; shake goes through `deps.shake`, which `CameraRig` scales by 0.25 and zeroes under Reduced Motion. Shake only for the involved local player. No new setting.
6. Rig: `packages/shared/src/rig.ts` is unchanged. m1.1 to m1.4, wall, pickup, throw, guard and dash clips already exist for all three kits (QF01 to QF03, T23); no generated asset changed, so content:validate and assets:audit were not rerun.
7. Not done: no mid-flight urn streak or endpoint telegraph for throw (QV01 limit retained); no wall-bounce ragdoll effect (no replicated wall contact). Counter, wall, pickup, throw, capture, release, KO and respawn in the browser case are server broadcast events on a synthetic fixture; the M1 chain, block, guard raise and dash are real input confirmed by the server.
8. QV07/QV08 polish notes (Horizon Break, Rift Palm, Echo Tap, Gale, Current Bind, Cascade Chain, Split Tide thin or small; Solar Vent blobs; Riptide trail) concern kit recipes owned by those rows. Only the Riptide trail overlaps this task: the new dash trail (real movement) is the evidence that fixture limits caused it. Kit recipe retunes are listed as remaining issues rather than edited here.
