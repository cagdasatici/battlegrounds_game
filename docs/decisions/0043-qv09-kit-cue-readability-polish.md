# 0043 QV09 follow-up: kit cue readability polish (delegated design calls)

Owner delegated design decisions to the orchestrator (decision 0033). Ratings are capture-based, delegated, not a human sign-off. Closes the polish notes carried from QV06-QV09 (decisions 0036, 0038, 0039, 0041). No ledger row; recorded as a follow-up section in docs/test-results/QV09.md.

1. Constraints kept: pooled particles only, no new geometry; every recipe stays at or below 100 particles at the low cap (pool 200); medium budget 120 calls / 150k triangles.
2. Beams (Horizon Break, Solar Vent, Furnace Ray): the beam body is now one continuous row of pooled spheres. Stations are evenly spaced along the aim; the odd emit is shifted by half a step (`beamStation`/`beamGen` in kits/common.ts), so two consecutive emits interleave and the union gap is len / (2n). The parity is an emit counter in the per-actor sustain state (not the tick), so a slow frame rate cannot repeat a parity. Core counts: Horizon 26 (was 8), Solar 26 (was 14), Furnace 20 (was 12). Sheath is a halo hugging the core (lateral 0.4 m for Solar, tapering 0.75 to 0.2 m for Furnace, 0.6 to 1.2 m for Horizon), not blobs 2 m to each side. Solar and Furnace cores grow with distance (x1.8 and x1.5 at the far end) so the far end still reads under perspective. Solar emit 5 ticks, life 0.16 s, so at most two emits are alive (peak count is exact). Flares trimmed (Solar 10 to 6, Furnace 8 to 6) and Horizon generic impact 12 to 8 to pay for the density.
3. Small cues: Rift Palm arc 10 to 18 particles at 0.65 m (was 0.34), centre line 4 to 6 at 0.85 m, life 0.4 s. Echo Tap tap ring 8 to 12 at 0.6 m, echo ring 10 to 16 at 0.5 m (neighbour spacing under 1.2x size), lives 0.3 / 0.6 s. Gale streaks 12 to 20 in five lanes at 0.55 m, life 0.4 s. Current Bind loop 12 to 18 at 0.4 m, lead 6 to 8 at 0.32 m. Cascade Chain 4 to 6 per link at 0.38 m. Split Tide probes 14 to 18 at 0.4 m and 22 to 26 at 0.55 m. Riptide trail 3 to 4 per step at 0.45 m (was 0.3), lunge ring 0.35 m.
4. Riptide fixture: the capture fixture now moves the caster 4 m through Active (16 steps of 0.25 m) and takes a `td.a4-trail` still mid-dash; td.a4 is added to the medium set. The case asserts more than the lunge ring is alive mid-dash. Position is still written through the same `place` helper; replication, pooling and cleanup are real.
5. Regression floors: unit tables assert minimum particle size, count, ring spacing and the beam gap rule (largest gap between interleaved core particles at most 75% of the core size, including under a cover clip and with emits two intervals apart), plus the 100 particle cap for every touched recipe. Two older assertions encoded the retired look (Furnace core thinner than its sheath; Solar sheath 2.2 m wide) and were replaced.
6. Captures: `QV09_POLISH=1` redirects the QV06/QV07/QV08 stills to `docs/test-results/QV09/polish/` so the original stills stay as the before set. The three cases share `low-report.json` / `medium-report.json` there, so the last run (QV08) wins; the stills are per move.
7. Limits: stills are SwiftShader desktop Chromium; a cue still can land on the tail of a short effect. The observer camera is side-back, so beams that run sideways leave the frame and bodies occlude close cues. Node 24 is not installed on this machine (Node 26 ran); physical iPad NOT RUN.
